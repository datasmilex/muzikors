# Muzikors - Product Context

## Overview
Muzikors is a social interactive jukebox and venue music management platform. In cafes, bars, clubs, and events, customers browse songs, upvote/boost their favorite tracks in real time, and request music directly from the venue's active Spotify sound system.

## Audience & Users
- **Primary Users:** Cafe/bar customers listening to music in venues, requesting songs, voting in the queue, engaging in the social feed, and customizing their profiles.
- **Environment:** Often used in dimly-lit social environments (bars, cafes, lounges) on mobile devices (iOS / Android / Web).
- **Core Mode:** **Operate & Experience** — Fast, scan-friendly, one-thumb reachability, instant tactile feedback, and crisp premium aesthetics.

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic Principles
- **Atmosphere:** Sophisticated, nightlife/lounge luxury, modern, clean, and warm.
- **Palette Identity:** Deep dark Obsidian base, rich Gold/Amber accents, Crema Gold luxury notes, and crisp emerald/ruby status indicators.
- **Anti-References (What We Reject - AI Slop):**
  - No generic purple/violet tech gradients (`from-purple-600`).
  - No clunky nested cards (cards inside cards inside cards).
  - No decorative text clipping gradients on standard headings.
  - No jerky/dated bounce animations (`animate-bounce`).
  - No low-contrast unreadable gray text on dark backgrounds.
  - No oversized clunky buttons or tiny untouchable touch targets.

## Key UX Pillars
1. **Speed & Ergonomics:** Song search, instant preview, voting, and boosting must be 1-tap actions.
2. **Clear Visual Hierarchy:** The Currently Playing track and Queue position must always dominate the primary viewport.
3. **Smooth Micro-interactions:** Tactile touch feedback (`active:scale-95`), smooth ease-out transitions, and clean typography.
